About Dr. Ankit Rai

Orthopedic, Trauma & Joint Replacement Surgeon

Dr. Ankit Rai holds an MBBS, MS in Orthopedics and FIJR from Shalby. He has 10 years of experience across Maharashtra, Gujarat and Delhi, caring for orthopedic and trauma patients.

His practice includes knee, hip and shoulder replacement, arthroscopic sports injury care, fracture management, limb lengthening and deformity correction. He also manages joint pain with appropriate non-surgical care.
