Assessment for fractures and trauma

Fractures and orthopedic trauma can affect children and adults. Dr. Ankit Rai assesses the injury, symptoms and imaging to plan care around the fracture and individual needs.

Dr. Rai's expertise includes orthopedic and pediatric trauma, infected trauma, failed surgery, deformity correction and Ilizarov surgery for limb lengthening.

Fracture and trauma treatment options

Care is planned according to the fracture, injury severity and individual assessment. For suitable cases, treatment may involve deformity correction or limb lengthening using Ilizarov techniques.

  • Orthopedic fracture assessment
  • Pediatric trauma management
  • Complex and infected trauma care
  • Failed surgery assessment
  • Ilizarov surgery and limb lengthening
  • Deformity correction
  • Injury-related mobility concerns
  • Care guided by individual assessment
