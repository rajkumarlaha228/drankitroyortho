Assessment for joint pain

Knee, hip or shoulder pain can affect walking, work, sport and daily activities. Dr. Ankit Rai assesses your symptoms, medical history and movement to understand the problem and discuss appropriate care.

Dr. Rai has 10 years of orthopedic experience and has treated more than 10,000 patients with conservative knee and hip management. When surgery is appropriate, care may include primary or revision joint replacement.

Treatment options for joint conditions

Care depends on the diagnosis, symptoms and individual needs. Options may include conservative management or, when clinically appropriate, knee, hip or shoulder replacement surgery. Your assessment helps guide the discussion.

  • Knee, hip and shoulder joint assessment
  • Conservative knee and hip management
  • Primary joint replacement surgery
  • Revision joint replacement surgery
  • Arthritis-related joint symptoms
  • Movement and mobility concerns
  • Treatment options explained clearly
  • Care guided by individual assessment
